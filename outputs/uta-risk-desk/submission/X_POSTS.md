## Option 1: the account moved

a collateral ratio can change your account even when your balance hasn't moved.

i built Rulewake for @Bitget_AI base camp s2.

it takes a reviewed UTA rule change, runs the account impact through deterministic math, then lets Qwen explain the validated trace.

in the demo, rSTRC moves from 90% to 85%. effective equity drops $5k. the margin ratio moves from 94.44% to 100%. the trader sees the buffer before deciding what to do.

no trades. no model-generated arithmetic. no API keys in the browser.
