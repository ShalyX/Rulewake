## Twenty-startup test

### Rulewake

The stepped downstream mark could appear in other rules or compliance products, but the combination of a rule bar, tier rows and changed continuation is tightly tied to this product's causal mechanism. Pass.

### Ratiofold

Folded before/after panels could fit many comparison products. Registration notches and ratio typography improve specificity, but the system depends more heavily on execution. Conditional pass.

### Tiertrace

Stepped traces are common in observability, workflow and analytics tools. The mark needs context to avoid looking like a developer product. Weak pass.

## Decision rule

Choose Rulewake unless one of these becomes true before submission:

1. a material same-category collision appears;
2. the desired public handle cannot be obtained and handle consistency becomes a launch requirement;
3. user testing repeatedly misreads the name as a marine product after seeing the wordmark and one-line descriptor.

If Rulewake fails, move to Ratiofold. Do not reopen the rejected-name pool.
