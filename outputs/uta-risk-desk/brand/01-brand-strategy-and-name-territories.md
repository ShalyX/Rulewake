# Brand strategy and naming territories

Updated: 19 September 2026  
Status: Rulewake selected on 19 September 2026; original visual treatment retired

## Brand brief

### Product essence

The product answers one narrow, consequential question:

> If Bitget changes this collateral rule, what changes in my actual UTA account before the rule takes effect?

It converts a generic platform notice into a personal, inspectable before/after state. Qwen reads and explains. Deterministic code owns every financial number. The user owns the decision.

### Mechanism worth naming

1. A verified Bitget rule changes.
2. One or more tiered collateral contributions change.
3. The effect propagates through a shared cross-margin account.
4. The product isolates that delta and shows the resulting equity, ratio, risk band and buffer.
5. The trace and worksheet make the result auditable.

### Desired perception

- exact, calm and useful under time pressure;
- independent without pretending to replace Bitget;
- technical enough to trust, legible enough to act on;
- a decision instrument, not a prediction engine;
- specific to change impact, not another generic trading dashboard.

### Verbal personality

Direct, measured and evidence-led. Short sentences for outcomes. Plain language for the explanation. No bravado, alpha claims, liquidation guarantees or anthropomorphic AI language.

## Cliche audit

The identity must not rely on:

- shields, locks, vaults or padlocks;
- candlesticks, line charts, arrows or bull/bear silhouettes;
- brains, sparkles, circuit traces or robot heads;
- globes, hexagons, coins or exchange-style letter monograms;
- black backgrounds with interchangeable cyan/purple gradients;
- green = safe / red = danger as the entire brand idea.

The product's ownable material is the **propagation of one verified rule change through one shared account**, plus the visible calculation trace.

## Territory A — Rulewake

### Core idea

A rule change leaves a measurable wake through the account. The identity captures cause and downstream consequence without using a market chart or alert icon.

### Name rationale

`Rulewake` is an invented compound: rule + wake. It says the product begins with an external rule and follows what the rule disturbs. It still works if the product later covers maintenance tiers, leverage rules or other account parameters.

### Visual primitive

One vertical rule intersects a set of calibrated rows. The rows continue at changed lengths after the rule, producing a stepped downstream profile. It can read as tiers, a ledger and a wake without illustrating any of them literally.

### Art direction

- Editorial instrument, not terminal cosplay.
- Warm calculation-paper ground.
- Dense near-black ink.
- Burnt orange for the changed term.
- Mineral green-grey for unchanged context.
- Condensed, assertive display typography paired with a neutral text face.

### Voice line

**See what the rule changes.**

### Product descriptor

Account impact for Bitget UTA collateral changes.

### Risks

- `rulewake.com` resolves and appears parked or undeveloped, so a `.com` acquisition is not assumed.
- The wake story must stay geometric; literal water graphics would make it feel like a maritime product.

## Territory B — Ratiofold

### Core idea

The product folds current and projected account states into one comparable, auditable object.

### Name rationale

`Ratiofold` is an invented compound: ratio + fold. It foregrounds the before/after comparison and the exported worksheet. The name is less dependent on the trigger and more dependent on the product artefact.

### Visual primitive

Two unequal panels meet on a fold. A small registration notch shows the exact point of comparison. The silhouette can become an app icon, worksheet stamp or before/after divider.

### Art direction

- A more editorial, document-led system.
- Vellum ground with aubergine ink.
- Blotter pink and cobalt as annotation colours.
- Large ratios, tight rules and visible registration marks.
- The exported worksheet is treated as a first-class brand surface.

### Voice line

**Before and after, on the same terms.**

### Product descriptor

Before/after margin impact for Bitget UTA.

### Risks

- “Ratio” may make the product sound narrower than its effective-equity and buffer work.
- “Fold” is a popular technology metaphor and needs a distinctive visual system to stay ownable.

## Territory C — Tiertrace

### Core idea

Trust comes from following the exact tier path that produced the answer.

### Name rationale

`Tiertrace` joins the product's progressive collateral tiers with its strongest proof mechanism: the expandable deterministic trace.

### Visual primitive

A single path crosses progressively offset tier rows, with open terminals that expose rather than hide the calculation route.

### Art direction

- Technical field manual rather than dashboard.
- Deep blue-black ink, powder-green paper and vermilion checkpoints.
- Monospaced numerical annotations used sparingly.
- Repeated row geometry becomes the system's pattern language.

### Voice line

**Follow every tier to the result.**

### Product descriptor

Traceable collateral-change impact for Bitget UTA.

### Risks

- The name leans toward developer tooling and could undersell the decision outcome.
- `TierTrace` already appears as a generic type name in a small Rust scoring library; this is not a product collision, but it weakens absolute uniqueness.

## Name decision

**Rulewake is locked.** It owns the complete causal story—external rule, downstream account consequence—rather than one output metric or one implementation detail. It also survives the rename and category-removal tests best: the sentence “see what the rule changes” still describes the product without saying AI, crypto, risk or Bitget.

The original Rulewake board is not the selected art direction. Its warm paper, editorial composition, palette and provisional stepped-row mark are retired. Ratiofold and Tiertrace remain naming-history only.

## Naming guardrails

- The public name stands alone; “UTA Risk Desk” never appears as a subtitle or fallback.
- `Bitget` appears in the descriptor and compatibility language, not inside the product name.
- Do not add `AI` to the wordmark. Qwen is a bounded capability, not the brand premise.
- Do not add “terminal,” “copilot,” “sentinel,” “guard,” “safe,” or “alpha.”
- Preliminary collision research is not legal trademark clearance.
