# Brand decision record

Name selected: **Rulewake**  
Selected: 19 September 2026  
Visual direction: deliberately reopened

## Locked name

### Rulewake

**See what the rule changes.**  
Account impact for Bitget UTA collateral changes.

The name is locked for its memorable causal story: a platform rule changes, then its effect moves through the account.

Domain caveat: the `.com` resolves and should not be assumed available.

## Rejected naming alternatives

### Ratiofold

**Before and after, on the same terms.**  
Before/after margin impact for Bitget UTA.

Best for: making the worksheet and comparison object the centre of the identity.

Trade-off: the name describes the output more than the platform-change trigger.

## Rejected technical alternative

### Tiertrace

**Follow every tier to the result.**  
Traceable collateral-change impact for Bitget UTA.

Best for: emphasising deterministic math, provenance and calculation trust.

Trade-off: it can sound like developer infrastructure.
